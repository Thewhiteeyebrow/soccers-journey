Share links

A link shows the trail and the stamps marked "link" or "public". Check-ins and crossroads stay private on a link unless you change them. Revoke a link and it dies at once.

The ledger

Each stamp is hashed into a chain. Every night the day's stamps are folded into one root that is published. Anyone can check that a stamp existed on a date; nobody can read it from the root.
